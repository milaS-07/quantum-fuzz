Of 199 benchmark circuits, 188 admitted at least one transformation and were tested (11 had no applicable move and are not evidence); 13 more were excluded (empty, too large or duplicate). The tested circuits have 3-10 qubits and 3-643 gates; 45965 distinct transformed circuits were generated (at least 1 per circuit, or all that the search could reach) and each was compared with its original: full unitary up to 6 qubits, random states above. Every one matched up to global phase (largest deviation 1.9e-10, tolerance 1e-07). In addition 150 random circuits (random2q, staggered, redundant, random3q, structured; 3-8 qubits) were tested. Over all 349 independent circuits, 6 failed, so the per-circuit failure probability is below 3.4% at 95% confidence (Clopper-Pearson). 68248 distinct transformed circuits and 91306 comparisons in total. Each move kind (block-2q, block-3q, window-2q, window-3q, peephole, and blocks with gates reordered in either direction) was exercised at least 442 times. The checker detected 8 of 8 deliberately injected bugs. Results are reproducible with a fixed seed across processes. optimize_circuit's best_circuit was equivalent to its input in 130 of 134 end-to-end runs.
