The equivalence suite was run with 30 independent seeds (0-29); every seed generates its own random circuits, check states, search walks and benchmark parameter values. 194 of 205 benchmark circuits (3-10 qubits, 3-643 gates) admitted at least one transformation. 4499 random circuits (random2q, staggered, redundant, random3q, structured) were generated in total, 149 per seed. 2094939 distinct transformed circuits (sum over seeds, overlaps possible) and 2797464 comparisons with their originals or parents were checked (full unitary up to 6 qubits, random states above); 1 failures, largest deviation 9.9e-09, tolerance 1e-07. Counting every generated circuit and each benchmark circuit once, 1 of 4704 independent circuits failed, so the per-circuit failure probability is below 0.101% at 95% confidence (Clopper-Pearson). Each move kind (block-2q, block-3q, window-2q, window-3q, peephole, and reordering in either direction) was exercised at least 16126 times in total. The checker detected 240 of 240 deliberately injected bugs across all seeds. Search output is identical across processes. optimize_circuit's best_circuit was equivalent to its input in 4020 of 4020 end-to-end runs.
